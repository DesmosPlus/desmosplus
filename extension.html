Desmos+

Graph transfer, SVG conversion, and audio creation tools for Desmos.

Desmos+ works with supported official Desmos calculators. It transfers complete graph state, converts static SVG artwork into editable equations, and creates playable Desmos audio graphs with DesAudify.

Features

  • Import and export portable .desmos graph files.
  • Preserve expressions, folders, tables, sliders, notes, and supported animation state.
  • Convert static SVG paths and shapes into editable Desmos equations.
  • Create audio graphs with Auto, High quality, MAX, and Custom conversion modes.
  • Download large audio projects as individually pasteable graph shards.
  • Process selected graph, SVG, and audio files locally in the browser.
